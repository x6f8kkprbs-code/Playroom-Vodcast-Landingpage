Formats

Podcast formats for brands and companies

Podcasts and video podcasts have become one of the most effective formats for long-term brand communication. They allow for conversations, perspectives and ideas that go beyond short social media posts.

Companies use podcast formats to make their expertise visible, communicate topics credibly and build a direct connection with their audience.

Video podcasts add a visual layer to the format. A single recording works as an audio podcast, as a video for platforms like YouTube or LinkedIn, and as social media content.

Studio

Podcast studio in Frankfurt

Playroom Studios is a podcast and video podcast studio in Frankfurt am Main with a recording room built specifically for high-quality voice recording.

The studio space is acoustically treated, so conversations stay clear – without room reflections or background noise.

This acoustic quality matters most in longer conversation formats and is what makes a recording sound professional from start to finish.

Six setups, one studio

Depending on format, conversation setting and audience, we arrange the studio to fit your production – from a relaxed conversation to an on-camera presentation.

Executive setup
A modular TV-studio setting with interchangeable background surfaces in different colors and materials. Every production gets its own look, matched to the format and the brand.
Lounge setup
Comfortable sofas, warm light and an open conversational atmosphere. For storytelling formats, CEO talks and conversations that are deliberately less formal.
Focus setup
A podcast table with professional microphones, accent lighting and multi-camera preparation. For interviews, expert formats and subject-driven conversations.
Armchair setup
Two armchairs, a focused visual language and a personal atmosphere. Ideal for interviews, deep dives and conversations that put people at the center.
Solo setup red
One person, a colored fabric wall, a clear frontal frame. For statements, explainers and social media formats.
Solo setup dark
A dark background with room for your own roll-up banner. For expert topics, product messaging and trade show content.

The setups shown are examples of typical production situations. Depending on format, number of participants and concept, we adapt the room, lighting, camera positions and equipment individually.

We advise on the design of the setting and make sure the production fits your content and goals.

Video podcast production in 4K

Alongside classic audio podcasts, Playroom Studios produces video podcasts with a professional multi-camera setup in 4K.

Picture and sound are matched precisely, resulting in formats that look polished and deliver the clear audio that longer conversations depend on.

Guests can also join remotely.
